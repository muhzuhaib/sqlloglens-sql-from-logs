# Privacy Policy

**SqlLogLens - Restore SQL from Logs**
Version 1.0, 24 September 2026
Vendor: **Muhammad Zuhaib Zahid**

## The short version

**The plugin collects nothing.** It has no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
network communication of its own. Your logs, the SQL restored from them and the values in that SQL
never leave your machine.

That matters here because a restored statement carries real values from your application's data:
customer names, email addresses, order totals, whatever your queries touch.

## What that means in detail

- **Restoring from a selection.** The text you select is read in your IDE's process, the statement is
  rebuilt there, and the result is put on your clipboard. Nothing else is kept.
- **Capture (SqlLogLens Pro).** The output of the processes your IDE runs is read as it is printed,
  and the statements found in it are kept in the SQL Log tool window. **They are held in memory only,
  never written to disk, and are gone when the project closes** or when you press Clear.
- **No usage tracking.** The plugin does not record which features you use or anything about your
  projects.
- **No accounts.** The plugin asks you for no personal information and has no sign-in of its own.
- **No third-party code.** The plugin bundles no library of anyone else's, so there is nothing else
  inside it that could send data anywhere.

## What the Vendor does receive

Only what you choose to send:

- **A purchase.** JetBrains sells SqlLogLens Pro as merchant of record and shares with the Vendor the
  sales information needed to be paid and to comply with tax law. What JetBrains collects at purchase
  is covered by the [JetBrains Privacy Policy](https://www.jetbrains.com/legal/docs/privacy/privacy.html).
- **Support you initiate.** If you email the Vendor or open an issue, the Vendor receives what you
  write, including any log you attach. **Please replace real values in a log before you attach it.**
  The shape of the lines is what matters for a bug report, not the data in them.

## Licence validation

Checking that a licence or trial is valid is performed by **the JetBrains IDE**, not by this plugin,
using JetBrains' own licensing service. That exchange is governed by JetBrains' terms and privacy
policy.
